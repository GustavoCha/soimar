# Soimar visual redesign notes

## Baseline audit

### Keep

- The diagnostic as the site's distinctive interactive proof.
- The direct, plain-language offer and WhatsApp-first contact path.
- The accessible comparison control, explorable work content, process, company enquiry, FAQ, and workshop location.
- Archivo's variable width as a recognizable typographic asset.

### Evolve

- Reduce the header to the decisions a visitor actually needs and make mobile contact persistent without obscuring content.
- Recompose the hero so the offer, action, and diagnostic read as one experience instead of two competing columns.
- Replace repeated card grids with editorial lists, ruled specifications, and alternating density.
- Turn the diagnostic into a calm, guided three-step instrument with explicit progress and result language.
- Make all demonstrative imagery visibly illustrative until verified Soimar photographs are available.

### Replace

- Remove the mockup information bar.
- Replace the oversized multi-line headline and provisional cityscape windshield illustration.
- Replace the accordion-like hover gallery on small screens with a keyboard- and touch-first case selector.
- Remove the simulated form success state; the preview will explain that sending is not yet connected.
- Replace invented or unconfirmed phone, map coordinates, schedules, and links with visible pending states or omissions.

## Visual directions considered

### Laminated precision — selected

The visual system comes from the windshield itself: mineral charcoal, safety-glass white, cool laminated blue, a restrained signal amber, fine frit dots, inspection marks, and ruled technical annotations. The first viewport behaves like a windshield inspection bench: the offer occupies the clear viewing area, while the diagnostic is the working proof rather than a separate card. Risk: it can become cold; this is offset with direct Chilean Spanish, generous daylight surfaces, and visible human guidance.

### Harbor workshop ledger — alternate

A local service ledger inspired by Valparaíso workshop tickets, port manifests, stamped dates, and compact handwritten annotations. It would feel close, local, and tactile, with navy ink, off-white stock, safety orange, and ledger rules. Risk: the editorial metaphor can delay the immediate repair decision and make a safety service feel nostalgic rather than current.

## Why the selected direction wins

It identifies the product even with all copy removed, makes the interactive diagnosis the commercial demonstration, scales cleanly from 375px to 1440px, and supports accessible states without relying on color alone. The patent-sheet challenger contributed disciplined leader lines and explicit state marks; the result retains that rigor without turning the site into a literal engineering drawing.

## Planned improvements

- One clear primary action in the first viewport, with secondary phone contact visibly subordinate.
- Shorter headline, tighter reading measure, and a more intentional responsive type scale.
- A single continuous visual grammar across navigation, diagnostic, comparison, services, cases, process, business, proof, FAQ, and location.
- Minimum 44px controls, visible focus, keyboard alternatives for drag, `aria-live` feedback, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support.
- No fabricated commercial proof, photos, reviews, certifications, or submission success.
